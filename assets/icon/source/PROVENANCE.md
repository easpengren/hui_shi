# Where the icon comes from

`luji-portrait.jpg` — 陸機 Lu Ji (261–303), the poet of the 文賦, from the anonymous
Qing dynasty leaf **陆机、陆云二像页** (Lu Ji and Lu Yun), Shanghai Museum.
3000 × 2259.

**Public domain** on Wikimedia Commons:
<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:佚名_陆机、陆云二像页_(陆机).jpg>

He is the Lu Ji whose 平復帖 calligraphy this app already carries
(ASSET_ATTRIBUTION.md), not 陸績, the filial son who shares the romanisation.
Framed like every other app in the suite (2026-09-26).
